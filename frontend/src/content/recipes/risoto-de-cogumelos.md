title_en: Perfect brownie
description_en: >-
  Perfect for selling, giving as a gift or making in advance, it stays fresh for

ingredients_en:
  - 175g semi-sweet chocolate in pieces
  - 150g unsalted butter
  - 30g cocoa powder
  - 3 eggs
  - 250g sugar
  - 140g wheat flour
materials_en:
  - 20x20 shape
  - baking paper
steps_en:
  - >-
    Line a 20x20 baking dish with baking paper and set aside. Meanwhile, preheat
    the oven to 180 degrees.
  - >-
    Melt the chocolate and butter in a bain marie or every 30 seconds in the
    microwave until melted. Be careful not to overheat and burn the chocolate.
  - >-
    Put the eggs and sugar in another bowl and beat with a whisk for 1 minute.
    If you like, you can add 5 g of vanilla extract at this stage.
  - >-
    Add the melted chocolate mixture, stir until smooth and finish with the
    flour, stirring quickly with a spatula just until the flour disappears.
  - >-
    Transfer to the tin and bake in a preheated oven at 180 degrees for about 35
    minutes.
